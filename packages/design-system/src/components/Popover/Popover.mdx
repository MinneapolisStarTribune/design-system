# Popover

Popovers provide lightweight, focused surfaces for quick actions or supplemental context. They open on click or focus, can include text, links, and simple actions, and support richer content through a flexible slot such as menu items or form controls. Popovers auto-size to their content, reposition as needed, and close when the user clicks outside, presses Escape, or completes an action.

## Anatomy

1. **Container** — The outer surface that holds the popover content and handles positioning and elevation.
2. **Close button (optional)** — Allows users to manually dismiss the popover. Hide it with `showCloseButton={false}`.
3. **Title (optional)** — Helps users understand the purpose of the popover.
4. **Description** — Primary supporting text.
5. **Header divider (optional)** — Separates header from content when needed.
6. **Custom content (optional)** — Flexible slot for menus, actions, or forms.
7. **Pointer** — Directional arrow connecting popover to trigger.

## Usage

- Use for contextual actions or supplementary information
- Keep content concise and scannable
- Use composition (`Popover.Heading`, `Popover.Body`, etc.) for layout control
- Add a divider when separating header and content
- Provide `aria-label` when no heading is present. With a heading, the heading names the popover and `aria-label` is ignored
- Avoid overcrowding content — prefer simple, focused interactions
- Use `className` for the popover surface and the subcomponents' className props for their sections

## Heading options

`Popover.Heading` also takes these props:

- `eyebrow` — a small label above the title, such as a date.
- `value` — content at the end of the title row, such as a total.
- `showCloseButton` — shows the close button. Defaults to `true`.

`eyebrow` and `value` are not part of the popover's accessible name. Only the title names it.

## Portal container

The popover renders into `document.body`. Pass `portalRoot` to render it into another element, such as a sidebar or a Storybook frame. Set it on each popover that needs it, the same way as `Drawer`.

## Example

```tsx
<Popover.Root trigger={<Button>Open</Button>} placement="bottom" className="custom-popover">
  <Popover.Heading className="gap-2">Title</Popover.Heading>

  <Popover.Description>Sample description</Popover.Description>

  <Popover.Divider />

  <Popover.Body className="custom-popover-body">
    {' '}
    <UtilityBody>Content</UtilityBody>
  </Popover.Body>
</Popover.Root>
```
